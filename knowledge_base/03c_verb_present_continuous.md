# Verb Tenses: The Present Continuous (ತತ್ಕ್ಷಣ / ನಡೆಯುತ್ತಿರುವ ಕ್ರಿಯೆ)

**Usage:** Actions happening *right now*, at this very moment.

- **Structure:** `Root` + `-tā` + `iru (conjugated)`

## The Formula

1. Take the verb root (e.g. **Baru** — to come)
2. Add **-tā** → **Bartā** (coming)
3. Add a conjugated form of "to be" (**iddīni / iddāne / …**)

So the *first* part (**Bartā**) never changes — only the **iru** ("to be") tail
conjugates for person. Learn the present tense of **iru** and you have every verb's
continuous form.

---

## Worked Example: Baru (ಬರು — to come) → **Bartā**

| Pronoun | Kannada (Spoken) | Transliteration | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nānu** | ನಾನು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ | **Nānu bartā iddīni** | I am coming |
| **Nīnu** | ನೀನು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದಿಯ | **Nīnu bartā iddiya** | You are coming |
| **Nīvu** | ನೀವು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀರಿ | **Nīvu bartā iddīri** | You (resp/pl) are coming |
| **Avanu** | ಅವನು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದಾನೆ | **Avanu bartā iddāne** | He is coming |
| **Avaḷu** | ಅವಳು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದಾಳೆ | **Avaḷu bartā iddāḷe** | She is coming |
| **Adu** | ಅದು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದೆ | **Adu bartā ide** | It is coming |
| **Nāvu** | ನಾವು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀವಿ | **Nāvu bartā iddīvi** | We are coming |
| **Avaru** | ಅವರು ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದಾರೆ | **Avaru bartā iddāre** | They are coming |

---

## The Continuous vs. the Habitual (don't mix them up!)

The continuous (**-tā iru**) is for *right now*; the plain present (**-tīni**) is for
habits or the future.

| Form | Kannada | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Habitual / Future | **Nānu māḍtīni** | I do / I will do |
| Continuous (now) | **Nānu māḍtā iddīni** | I am doing (right now) |

## Example Sentences

- **ಮಳೆ ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದೆ.** — *Maḷe bartā ide.* — It is raining (*rain is coming*).
- **ನಾನು ಊಟ ಮಾಡ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ.** — *Nānu ūṭa māḍtā iddīni.* — I am eating.
- **ಅವರು ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದಾರೆ.** — *Avaru kelasa māḍtā iddāre.* — They are working.
