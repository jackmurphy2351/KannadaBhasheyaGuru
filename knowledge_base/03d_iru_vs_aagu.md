# Verb Tenses: Iru vs Āgu — To Be / To Become (ಇರು ಮತ್ತು ಆಗು)

These two verbs confuse almost every learner. Both can translate as "to be" in English,
but they cover completely different ideas.

- **Iru (ಇರು)** = existence / location / possession ("to be *there*", "to have")
- **Āgu (ಆಗು)** = to happen / to become / to be finished (events, change of state)

---

## A. Iru (ಇರು) — Existence / Location

Used for "I am here," "I have," "It is in the box."

- **Present:** ನಾನು ಇಲ್ಲೇ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ. — *Nānu illē iddīni.* — I am right here.
- **Past:** ನಾನು ನಿನ್ನೆ ಊರಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೆ. — *Nānu ninne ūralli idde.* — I was in town yesterday.

### Present of Iru (the backbone of the Present Continuous)

| Pronoun | Kannada | Transliteration | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nānu** | ಇದ್ದೀನಿ | **iddīni** | I am / I exist |
| **Nīnu** | ಇದ್ದಿಯ | **iddiya** | You are |
| **Nīvu** | ಇದ್ದೀರಿ | **iddīri** | You (resp/pl) are |
| **Avanu** | ಇದ್ದಾನೆ | **iddāne** | He is |
| **Avaḷu** | ಇದ್ದಾಳೆ | **iddāḷe** | She is |
| **Adu** | ಇದೆ | **ide** | It is |
| **Nāvu** | ಇದ್ದೀವಿ | **iddīvi** | We are |
| **Avaru** | ಇದ್ದಾರೆ | **iddāre** | They are |

---

## B. Āgu (ಆಗು) — To Happen / To Become

Used for events, feelings, changes of state, or finishing things.

- **Present/Future:** ಅದು ಆಗುತ್ತೆ. — *Adu āgutte.* — It will happen / It happens.
- **Past:** ಅದು ಆಯ್ತು. — *Adu āytu.* — It happened / It is finished.

### Becoming something (Noun/Adjective + Āgu)

- ನಾನು ಡಾಕ್ಟರ್ ಆಗ್ತೀನಿ. — *Nānu ḍākṭar āgtīni.* — I will become a doctor.
- ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ಆಯ್ತು. — *Cennāgi āytu.* — It turned out well.

---

## Conversational Gem

When Kannadigas finish a task (eating, working), they don't say "I finished." They say
**"it happened"** — using Āgu, not Iru.

- **ಊಟ ಆಯ್ತಾ?** — *Ūṭa āytā?* — *Literal:* Did the meal happen? → **Have you eaten?**
- **ಹೂಂ, ಆಯ್ತು.** — *Hūn, āytu.* — *Literal:* Yes, it happened. → **Yes, I ate.**

> Rule of thumb: if you can replace "to be" with "to exist / to be located," use **Iru**.
> If you can replace it with "to happen / to become / to get done," use **Āgu**.
