# Kannada Verbs — The Basics (ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದಗಳ ಮೂಲಗಳು)

This is the shared foundation for every verb-tense lesson. The pronominal endings
("the tail") below are reused across all tenses, so learn them once here.

## The Golden Formula

In Kannada, mastering the verb is most of the battle. Once you know the root and the
ending, you can construct almost any sentence.

`Verb Root` + `Tense Marker` + `Pronominal Ending` = **Conjugated Verb**

Because the pronominal ending already tells you *who* is acting, the subject pronoun
(Nānu, Nīnu…) can often be dropped and you will still be understood.

---

## The Pronominal Endings (The "Tail")

These suffixes tell you *who* is doing the action. Most tenses attach a version of
these to the stem. There is a more formal/written set and a colloquial/spoken set.

| Person | Pronoun | Meaning | Ending (Formal/Written) | Ending (Colloquial/Spoken) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Sing** | **Nānu** (ನಾನು) | I | **-ēne** (-ಏನೆ) | **-īni** (-ಈನಿ) |
| **2nd Sing** | **Nīnu** (ನೀನು) | You (informal) | **-iya** (-ಇಯ) | **-ya** / **-i** (-ಯ/-ಇ) |
| **2nd Hon** | **Nīvu** (ನೀವು) | You (polite/pl) | **-iri** (-ಇರಿ) | **-tīri** (-ತೀರಿ) |
| **3rd Masc** | **Avanu** (ಅವನು) | He | **-āne** (-ಆನೆ) | **-āne** (-ಆನೆ) |
| **3rd Fem** | **Avaḷu** (ಅವಳು) | She | **-āḷe** (-ಆಳೆ) | **-āḷe** (-ಆಳೆ) |
| **3rd Neut** | **Adu** (ಅದು) | It | **-ade** (-ಅದೆ) | **-utte** (-ಉತ್ತೆ) |
| **1st Pl** | **Nāvu** (ನಾವು) | We | **-ēve** (-ಏವೆ) | **-tīvi** (-ತೀವಿ) |
| **3rd Pl** | **Avaru** (ಅವರು) | They (human) | **-āre** (-ಆರೆ) | **-tāre** (-ತಾರೆ) |

> Note: Spoken forms vary by region. The colloquial column reflects common Bangalore/
> Mysore usage.

## The Three Core Tense Markers (at a glance)

| Tense | Marker | Example (root **Māḍu**, "to do") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present / Non-Past** | **-utta** | Māḍuttēne / Māḍtīni — *I do / I will do* |
| **Past** | **-d-** (+ irregulars) | Māḍide — *I did* |
| **Present Continuous** | **-tā + iru** | Māḍtā iddīni — *I am doing* |

Each marker gets its own focused lesson. This summary is just a map.

## Cross-Tense Summary: "To Drink" (Kuḍi, ಕುಡಿ)

A single verb shown across the tenses, so you can feel the pattern before drilling each one.

| Tense | Kannada | Transliteration | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past** | ನಾನು ಕುಡಿದೆ | **Nānu kuḍide** | I drank |
| **Present Continuous** | ನಾನು ಕುಡಿತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ | **Nānu kuḍitā iddīni** | I am drinking |
| **Habitual/Future** | ನಾನು ಕುಡಿತೀನಿ | **Nānu kuḍitīni** | I drink / I will drink |
| **Negative (Any time)** | ನಾನು ಕುಡಿಯಲ್ಲ | **Nānu kuḍiyalla** | I don't/won't drink |
| **Past Negative** | ನಾನು ಕುಡಿಯಲಿಲ್ಲ | **Nānu kuḍiyalilla** | I didn't drink |
