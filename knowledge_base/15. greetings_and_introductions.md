# Greetings & Introductions (ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಪರಿಚಯ)

Your first Kannada conversations will almost always follow the same short
script: greet, ask how someone is, trade names, say where you live, and take
your leave politely. Learn these as **whole phrases** first — the grammar
behind them comes later.

---

## 1. Saying Hello

| Kannada | Roman | Meaning | When |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ | *namaskāra* | Hello / greetings | Anyone, any time — always safe |
| ನಮಸ್ತೆ | *namaste* | Hello | Also fine, slightly less common in Karnataka |
| ಹಲೋ | *halō* | Hello | Casual, on the phone |

There is no separate "good morning" in everyday speech — ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ covers it.

---

## 2. Respectful vs. Familiar "You"

Kannada has two words for *you*, and choosing the right one matters more
than perfect grammar:

* **ನೀವು** (*nīvu*) — respectful. Use it with adults, elders, strangers,
  shopkeepers, colleagues. **When in doubt, use ನೀವು.**
* **ನೀನು** (*nīnu*) — familiar. Only for children, close friends, and
  younger siblings.

The possessive follows the same split: **ನಿಮ್ಮ** (*nimma*, your — respectful)
and **ನಿನ್ನ** (*ninna*, your — familiar).

* ನಿಮ್ಮ ಊರು ಯಾವುದು? (*Nimma ūru yāvudu?*) — Which is your home town?
* ನಿನ್ನ ಶಾಲೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (*Ninna śāle ellide?*) — Where is your school? (to a child)

---

## 3. "How are you?" and Answers

* ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (*Hēgiddīri?*) — How are you? (respectful)
* ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀಯ? (*Hēgiddīya?*) — How are you? (familiar)
* ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದ್ದೀನಿ, ನೀವು? (*Chennāgiddīni, nīvu?*) — I'm well, and you?

A very common Karnataka greeting is to ask whether someone has eaten:

* ತಿಂಡಿ ಆಯ್ತಾ? (*Tiṇḍi āytā?*) — Have you had breakfast/a snack?
* ಹೂಂ, ಆಯ್ತು. (*Hūṃ, āytu.*) — Yes, done.

It is not an invitation to lunch — it is simply "how's your day going?"

---

## 4. Names — No Word for "is"

Kannada often needs **no verb at all** where English uses *is*:

* ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಮೀನಾ. (*Nanna hesaru Mīnā.*) — My name [is] Meena.
* ಇವರು ನನ್ನ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತರು. (*Ivaru nanna snēhitaru.*) — This [is] my friend.

Colloquially people often add **ಅಂತ** (*anta*, "so-called"):
ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಮೀನಾ ಅಂತ.

## 5. Where You Live and Where You Are From

* ನಾನು ಮಲ್ಲೇಶ್ವರದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ. (*Nānu Mallēśvaradalli iddīni.*) — I live (am) in Malleshwaram.
  * **-ಅಲ್ಲಿ** (*-alli*) means *in / at*.
* ನೀವು ಯಾವ ದೇಶದವರು? (*Nīvu yāva dēśadavaru?*) — Which country are you from?
* ನಾನು ಅಮೆರಿಕದವನು / ಅಮೆರಿಕದವಳು. (*Nānu Amerikadavanu / Amerikadavaḷu.*) —
  I am from America (man / woman).

---

## 6. Survival Sentences for Learners

* ನಾನು ಈಗ ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ. — I am learning Kannada now.
* ನನಗೆ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕನ್ನಡ ಬರುತ್ತೆ. (*Nanage svalpa svalpa Kannaḍa barutte.*) — I know a little Kannada.
  * Literally "a little Kannada **comes to me**" — a language *comes to* you (ನನಗೆ, *to me*).
* ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಹೇಳಿ. (*Nidhānavāgi hēḷi.*) — Say it slowly.

---

## 7. Saying Goodbye

Kannada speakers avoid a bare "I'm going" — it sounds final. Instead:

* ಹೋಗಿ ಬನ್ನಿ. (*Hōgi banni.*) — "Go and come back" — said **to** someone leaving.
* ಸಿಗೋಣ. (*Sigōṇa.*) — "Let's meet (again)" — see you.
