# Verb Tenses: The Present Tense / Non-Past (ವರ್ತಮಾನ ಕಾಲ)

**Usage:** Habits, general truths, and near-future actions.
*In spoken Kannada there is rarely a separate "Future Tense." Saying "I go to the shop"
(Hōgtīni) already implies you will go.*

- **The Marker:** **-utta** (-ಉತ್ತ)
- **Structure:** `Root` + `utta` + `Ending`

---

## Worked Example 1: Māḍu (ಮಾಡು — to do)

| Pronoun | Written/Formal | Spoken/Conversational | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nānu** | **Māḍuttēne** | **Māḍtīni** | I do / I will do |
| **Nīnu** | **Māḍuttīya** | **Māḍtiya** | You do |
| **Nīvu** | **Māḍuttīri** | **Māḍtīri** | You (resp/pl) do |
| **Avanu** | **Māḍuttāne** | **Māḍtāne** | He does |
| **Avaḷu** | **Māḍuttāḷe** | **Māḍtāḷe** | She does |
| **Adu** | **Māḍuttade** | **Māḍutte** | It does |
| **Nāvu** | **Māḍuttēve** | **Māḍtīvi** | We do |
| **Avaru** | **Māḍuttāre** | **Māḍtāre** | They do |

## Example Sentences

- **ನಾನು ದಿನಾ ಕಾಫಿ ಕುಡಿತೀನಿ.** — *Nānu dinā kāfi kuḍitīni.* — I drink coffee every day.
- **ಅವನು ಆಫೀಸಿಗೆ ಹೋಗ್ತಾನೆ.** — *Avanu āphīsige hōgtāne.* — He goes to the office.
- **ನಾವು ನಾಳೆ ಸಿನಿಮಾ ನೋಡ್ತೀವಿ.** — *Nāvu nāḷe sinimā nōḍtīvi.* — We will watch a movie tomorrow.

## Quick Tip for Hindi Speakers

- **Habitual (*-tā*):** Just as Hindi uses **-tā** for habits (*Main kar**tā** hoon*),
  Kannada uses **-utta** (*Nānu māḍ**utta** ēne* → *Māḍuttēne* → *Māḍtīni*).
- **Continuous (*-rahā*):** Be careful! If you elongate the sound to **-uttā** and add
  *iddīni*, it becomes Continuous (*Main kar **rahā** hoon*). See the Present Continuous lesson.
  - *Māḍtīni* = *Kartā hoon* (I do)
  - *Māḍtā iddīni* = *Kar rahā hoon* (I am doing)
