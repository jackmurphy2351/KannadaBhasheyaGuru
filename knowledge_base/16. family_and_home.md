# Family & Home (ಕುಟುಂಬ ಮತ್ತು ಮನೆ)

Family is the first thing a Kannada speaker will ask you about. Kannada has
**separate words for elder and younger siblings**, and it shows respect to
elders through grammar — both are worth getting right early.

---

## 1. Family Words

| Kannada | Roman | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ಅಮ್ಮ / ತಾಯಿ | *amma / tāyi* | mother (everyday / formal) |
| ಅಪ್ಪ / ತಂದೆ | *appa / tande* | father (everyday / formal) |
| ಅಣ್ಣ | *aṇṇa* | **elder** brother |
| ತಮ್ಮ | *tamma* | **younger** brother |
| ಅಕ್ಕ | *akka* | **elder** sister |
| ತಂಗಿ | *taṅgi* | **younger** sister |
| ಅಜ್ಜ / ಅಜ್ಜಿ | *ajja / ajji* | grandfather / grandmother |
| ಗಂಡ / ಹೆಂಡತಿ | *gaṇḍa / heṇḍati* | husband / wife |
| ಮಗ / ಮಗಳು | *maga / magaḷu* | son / daughter |
| ಮಕ್ಕಳು | *makkaḷu* | children |

There is no single word for "brother" or "sister": you must pick elder or younger.

---

## 2. Respect Is Grammar

For elders and anyone you respect, use the **respectful plural** even for one person:

* **ಇವರು** (*ivaru*, this person — respectful), never **ಇದು** (*idu*, this thing).
* Verb ending **-ಆರೆ** (*-āre*): ಅಜ್ಜ ತೋಟದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದಾರೆ. (*Ajja tōṭadalli iddāre.*) — Grandfather is in the garden.

* ಇವರು ನನ್ನ ಅಜ್ಜ. (*Ivaru nanna ajja.*) — This is my grandfather.

---

## 3. "I have…" — Possession with ನನಗೆ

Kannada has no verb *to have*. Instead, something **exists to you**:
dative **ನನಗೆ** (*nanage*, to me) + **ಇದೆ / ಇದ್ದಾನೆ / ಇದ್ದಾಳೆ / ಇದ್ದಾರೆ** (is/are).

* ನನಗೆ ಒಬ್ಬ ತಂಗಿ ಇದ್ದಾಳೆ. (*Nanage obba taṅgi iddāḷe.*) — I have one younger sister.
* ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ಬೆಕ್ಕು ಇದೆ. (*Nanage ondu bekku ide.*) — I have a cat.

**Counting people** uses special words: ಒಬ್ಬ / ಒಬ್ಬಳು (*obba / obbaḷu*, one man / woman),
ಇಬ್ಬರು (*ibbaru*, two people), ಮೂವರು (*mūvaru*, three people). For things, use
ಒಂದು, ಎರಡು, ಮೂರು (*ondu, eraḍu, mūru*).

## 4. Home and Where You Live

| Kannada | Roman | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ಮನೆ | *mane* | house, home |
| ಅಡುಗೆಮನೆ | *aḍugemane* | kitchen ("cooking-house") |
| ಕೋಣೆ | *kōṇe* | room |
| ಊರು | *ūru* | home town, village |

Add **-ಅಲ್ಲಿ** (*-alli*) for *in*: ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ (*maneyalli*, at home), ಕೋಣೆಯಲ್ಲಿ (*kōṇeyalli*, in the room).

* ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆ ಬಸವನಗುಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿದೆ. (*Namma mane Basavanaguḍiyalli ide.*) — Our house is in Basavanagudi.
* Kannada speakers say **ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆ** (*namma mane*, our house) even when talking about their own home.

---

## 5. Useful Questions

* ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾರು ಯಾರು ಇದ್ದಾರೆ? (*Nimma maneyalli yāru yāru iddāre?*) — Who all lives in your house?
* ನಿಮ್ಮ ಅಪ್ಪ ಏನು ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾರೆ? (*Nimma appa ēnu māḍuttāre?*) — What does your father do?
* ನಿಮಗೆ ಮಕ್ಕಳು ಇದ್ದಾರಾ? (*Nimage makkaḷu iddārā?*) — Do you have children?
