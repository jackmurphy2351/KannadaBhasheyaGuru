# Verb Tenses: The Past Tense (ಭೂತ ಕಾಲ)

**Usage:** Completed actions.
- **The Marker:** usually **-d-** (-ದ), but common verbs are irregular.

The past tense uses a **short** set of endings (e.g. the "I" form is just **-e**, not
**-ēne**). 1st and 2nd person singular look identical (both **-e**).

---

## Group A: Regular Verbs (the 'u' verbs)

Most verbs ending in 'u' take a **-d-** sound to form the past stem.

- **Māḍu** (to do) → past stem **Māḍid-**

### Full Paradigm: Māḍu (ಮಾಡು — to do) → stem **Māḍid-**

| Pronoun | Written/Formal | Spoken | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nānu** | **Māḍide** | Māḍde | I did |
| **Nīnu** | **Māḍide** | Māḍde | You did |
| **Nīvu** | **Māḍidiri** | Māḍdri | You (resp/pl) did |
| **Avanu** | **Māḍidanu** | Māḍda | He did |
| **Avaḷu** | **Māḍidaḷu** | Māḍdaḷu | She did |
| **Adu** | **Māḍitu** | Māḍtu | It did |
| **Nāvu** | **Māḍidevu** | Māḍdvi | We did |
| **Avaru** | **Māḍidaru** | Māḍdru | They did |

---

## Group B: Irregular / Strong Verbs

These must be memorized — but they are the most common verbs in the language.

| Verb Root | Meaning | Past Stem | Example (I …) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Māḍu** | To do | **Māḍid-** | **Nānu māḍide** (I did) |
| **Baru** | To come | **Band-** | **Nānu bande** (I came) |
| **Hōgu** | To go | **Hōd-** | **Nānu hōde** (I went) |
| **Tinnu** | To eat | **Tind-** | **Nānu tinde** (I ate) |
| **Iru** | To be | **Idd-** | **Nānu idde** (I was) |
| **Kāṇu** | To see | **Kaṇḍ-** | **Nānu kaṇḍe** (I saw) |
| **Koḍu** | To give | **Koṭṭ-** | **Nānu koṭṭe** (I gave) |

### Full Paradigm: Hōgu (ಹೋಗು — to go) → stem **Hōd-**

| Pronoun | Written/Formal | Spoken | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nānu** | **Hōde** | Hōde | I went |
| **Nīnu** | **Hōde** | Hōde | You went |
| **Nīvu** | **Hōdiri** | Hōdri | You (resp/pl) went |
| **Avanu** | **Hōdanu** | Hōda | He went |
| **Avaḷu** | **Hōdaḷu** | Hōdaḷu | She went |
| **Adu** | **Hōyitu** | Hōytu | It went *(irregular neuter)* |
| **Nāvu** | **Hōdevu** | Hōdvi | We went |
| **Avaru** | **Hōdaru** | Hōdru | They went |

> Note the irregular **neuter** form **Hōyitu** ("it went") — the neuter "Adu" form is
> often the most irregular cell in the table.

---

## Example Sentences

- **ನಾನು ನಿನ್ನೆ ಮಾರ್ಕೆಟ್‌ಗೆ ಹೋದೆ.** — *Nānu ninne mārkeṭge hōde.* — I went to the market yesterday.
- **ಅವಳು ಊಟ ಮಾಡಿದಳು.** — *Avaḷu ūṭa māḍidaḷu.* — She ate the meal.
- **ಅವರು ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನಿಂದ ಬಂದರು.** — *Avaru beṅgaḷūrininda bandaru.* — They came from Bangalore.
